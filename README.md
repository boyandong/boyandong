# Boyan Dong

**Biomedical Engineering at Southeast University · Biosignals · Wearable Human–Machine Interaction**

I work on reliable biosignal interfaces, human-state sensing and wearable interaction systems. My projects connect research-question formulation, controlled evaluation and system integration, with explicit records of negative results and unresolved limitations.

## Featured research

| Project | Research question | Current evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CommonPose](https://github.com/boyandong/common-pose) | Do downstream classifier decisions remain consistent when EMG-predicted hand pose replaces measured pose? | Frozen evaluations and manuscript materials; decision agreement does not establish action correctness or deployment readiness. |
| [EEG Vigilance Reliability](https://github.com/boyandong/eeg-vigilance-reliability) | Can EEG support reliable functional prediction and adaptation for an unseen user? | Separate functional-prediction and adaptation branches; negative results and mechanism uncertainties are documented. |
| [HumanEngine](https://github.com/boyandong/emg-representation-learning) | How can causal EMG representations be learned and independently evaluated? | Research in progress. Infrastructure acceptance does not establish scientific validation of HumanEngine. |

## Product exploration and history

[CoCanvas](https://github.com/boyandong/campus-team-matching-app) is an archived campus team-matching app project. Its [demand-validation prototype](https://github.com/boyandong/campus-team-matching-validation) explores a fake-door testing approach using simulated listings and interaction logging. The prototype and proposed test plan alone do not demonstrate completed user validation or product demand.

## How I approach projects

I distinguish implemented software, executed experiments, validated findings and open hypotheses. Each repository documents its own scope, reproducibility instructions and limitations; historical artifacts retain their original context.
